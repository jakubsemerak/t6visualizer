normal roof, sliding door right, tailgate without glass, 3 front seats (driver + double passenger bench),
factory 3-seat row-2 bench (fixed) + partition with window behind row 2, GVW 3.0 t, N1 (LKW) registration.

## Coordinate system (VW, used 1:1 in the app)

- X: rearward, X=0 at front axle centre; rear axle X=3000 (SWB).
- Y: positive to the right (sliding-door side on LHD).
- Z: Z0 ≈ wheel-centre height; driver SgRP Z=610 = 942 mm above loaded road ⇒ road ≈ Z −330.

## Constraints

- Non-factory seats: 3-point belts (UNECE R14), seats/belts R17/R16; keep factory H-point; never mount on wheel
  arch; extra anchorages = converter's responsibility (76/115/EEC) [ABR §2.4.2, §3.2.2.2].
- Motorhome (EU 2018/858 SA): seats + table, bed, cooking, storage, all fixed. UK DVLA: bed ≥ 1800.
- Front double bench does not swivel as standard. Offset double swivel (Kiravans) is RHD only; many converters
  fit a single passenger swivel instead.
- Front axle ≥ 36 % of actual gross weight; avoid one-sided loading; weigh finished van (+5 % tolerance) [ABR].
- California SWB (3080 kg GVW) payload 444–701 kg.
- Pop-top raises roof by ≈ 1050; standing height up to ≈ 2300.
